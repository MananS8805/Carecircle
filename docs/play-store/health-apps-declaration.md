# Health apps declaration (draft)

Answers for the Play Console "Health apps" form. Verify against the current form.

- Category: Medical reference / health management. The app does not diagnose, treat or claim to detect any condition.
- It does not read from sensors or Health Connect. Health readings are typed in by the user and are never labelled normal or abnormal.
- Medicine and insurance text is informational, shows its sources (public label text, or the user's own policy clauses), and carries
  "Informational only, not medical advice" or "Informational only. Verify with your insurer. This is not a claim decision."
- Regulated-device status: none claimed. Have this reviewed before submission.
