The short version

CareCircle keeps your medicines, doses, notes and health readings on your phone. It sends a little information to a server only when you use a feature that needs it, and the table below lists every case. Nothing is sold, there is no advertising and no analytics.

CareCircle is informational. It is not medical advice and it is not an insurance claim decision.

What stays on your phone

People and their names, medicines, doses and dose history, side-effect diary, allergies, health readings (blood pressure, sugar, pulse, weight, temperature), appointments, refill history, saved insurance summaries, caregiver and pharmacy phone numbers, settings, and the reminder refresh log. Photos of medicine strips and of insurance papers are read on the phone and are not kept or uploaded. If the live camera cannot start, the phone's camera app writes one temporary picture that is read and deleted at once.

Diagnostics

Settings > Diagnostics shows a report of app and device details, whether sync is set up, the reminder permissions and the last 50 events (codes such as "status sent", never text). It stays on the phone. It leaves only if you tap Copy or Share. An optional crash log (off by default) records the error type and where it happened, not what was on screen. Delete all my data removes it.

What the server logs

For each request: the endpoint name, the status, how long it took, whether the answer came from cache and token counts. It does not log what you sent, medicine names, questions, document text or IP addresses. Network providers and Vercel keep their own infrastructure logs under their policies.

What is never sent

Photos, caregiver and pharmacy phone numbers, allergies, health readings, appointments, notes, refill history, and the contents of a backup file.

Your controls

  • Settings > Delete all my data removes everything on the phone, stops sharing, and cancels reminders.
  • Remove a person from Family sync to delete what was shared about them.
  • Export a backup, or restore one, from Settings. A backup stays in the place you save it, and you can lock it with a password (a lost password cannot be recovered).
  • Each upload of insurance text or a policy question needs your confirmation on a screen that says what will be sent.

Children

CareCircle is meant for adults managing medicines for themselves or for family members.
